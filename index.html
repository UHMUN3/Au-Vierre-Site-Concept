CARGO RUCKSACK

THE CARGO RUCKSACK IS MADE FROM 100% COTTON AND DESIGNED AROUND A RAW, UTILITARIAN SILHOUETTE. DISTRESSING IS APPLIED THROUGHOUT AS A VISUAL ELEMENT, ADDING TEXTURE AND EDGE WITHOUT ALTERING THE CORE CONSTRUCTION.

THREE FRONT CARGO POCKETS ARE FINISHED WITH BRASS HARDWARE. A DEDICATED WATER CANISTER POCKET FEATURES A BRASS EYELET FOR DRAINAGE. THE LAYOUT IS DIRECT AND FUNCTION-LED, WITH EVERY POCKET PLACED FOR ACCESS AND CARRY.

BANDOLEER-STYLE STRAPS RUN OVER THE SHOULDERS AND ARE DESIGNED TO HOLD SHOTGUN SHELLS, BRINGING A HARD, MILITARY-INFLUENCED DETAIL INTO THE DESIGN. THE RESULT IS A CLEAN BUT AGGRESSIVE RUCK WITH A STRIPPED-DOWN, TACTICAL PRESENCE.

CARGO BELLS

THE CARGO BELL-BOTTOM DENIM IS BUILT FROM HEAVY 14OZ 100% COTTON AND FINISHED WITH A DIRTY WASH THAT LOOKS LIVED-IN, NOT FAKED. DISTRESSING IS LEFT ROUGH AND INTENTIONAL, GIVING THE PIECE A WORN, HARD-USED CHARACTER FROM DAY ONE.

BRASS HARDWARE AND A BUTTON FLY ANCHOR THE PANT IN OLD-WORLD WORKWEAR, WHILE CARGO POCKETS SET INTO THE FLARE AND A NETTED BACK POCKET PUSH IT INTO UNFAMILIAR TERRITORY. A PATCH DETAIL HITS THE BACK AS A QUIET MARK OF IDENTITY.

THE 15.5-INCH LEG OPENING DRAGS LOW AND WIDE, DESIGNED TO BREAK OVER BOOTS AND COLLECT DIRT, NOT AVOID IT. EIGHT TOTAL POCKETS TURN FUNCTION INTO STATEMENT.
